Our Story

From a Small Kitchen Cart to Your Favorite Neighborhood Scoop Shop

What began with a vintage hand-crank freezer and a passion for wholesome ingredients has grown into Harry Ice Cream. We believe great ice cream doesn’t need artificial preservatives or fillers.

Every flavor is churned slowly to achieve a dense, velvety texture that melts just right. Whether you love timeless vanilla bean or adventurous spicy dark chocolate, there’s a scoop here with your name on it.
